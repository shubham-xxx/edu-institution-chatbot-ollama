At Chitkara Business School, what sets our MBA apart is the integrated Minor in Artificial Intelligence

in Business, offered across all specialisations. This pioneering five-course, 10-credit minor empowers
students with cutting-edge skills in Al, machine learning, generative tools and automation. With practical
exposure to platforms like ChatGPT, Copilot and intelligent agents, our students gain the ability to drive
innovation, streamline processes and make data-backed decisions—preparing them to lead in an
Al-powered business landscape.

In today’s data-driven economy, the ability to harness Artificial Intelligence is no longer optional — it’s essential.
The Minor in Artificial Intelligence in Business equips MBA students with practical expertise in Al, automation
and generative tools. This minor enables MBA students to complement their domain expertise in areas like
marketing, finance, HR or supply chain with Al competencies that are transforming business practices worldwide,
which in turn, enhances their career prospects in a technology-driven world,

From enhancing customer insights to automating workflows, graduates will be equipped to add strategic value
in any role. You will also learn to interpret data more intelligently and make faster, more informed decisions
across business functions. The minor, thus, promotes a mindset of continuous innovation, adaptability and
digital leadership.

Beyond foundational Al and Machine Learning concepts, the curriculum dives into the use of generative

Al tools, intelligent agents and ethical Al frameworks. With direct exposure to tools such as ChatGPT, Copilot
and RAG-based systems, students gain practical skills to solve real-world business challenges. This minor
gives our graduates a competitive edge in the job market and prepares them to lead innovation in any
organisation they join.
