MBA in Healthcare Management
with Minor in Artificial Intelligence for Business

Our MBA in Healthcare Management is designed to provide students with advanced
business management skills required to organise and manage complex healthcare
delivery systems. The program offers specialisations in Hospital Management

Digital Health Management and Public Health Management.

India’s healthcare industry is one of
the fastest-growing sectors, driven
by expanding infrastructure, greater
insurance coverage, rising health
awareness, medical technology
innovation and a growing focus on
wellness and preventive care. With
India’s status as a hub for medical
tourism, this growth is translating
into a surge of career opportunities
for skilled professionals across the
healthcare spectrum.

The program’s all-inclusive
curriculum adopts an integrated
approach, covering Hospitals,
Pharmaceuticals, Medical Devices,
Health Insurance, Healthcare
Financing, Public Health and
Healthcare Information Technology.
It is designed to strengthen functional
and leadership skills while deepening
the understanding of healthcare
organisations through projects,
dissertations and field visits.

Graduates of this program are
equipped to lead and innovate in
healthcare, with the ability to analyse
and manage data, optimise
operations and guide diverse teams.

The curriculum builds a strong
foundation in finance,
communication, human resources,
leadership, operations and critical
thinking, all contextualised to
healthcare and compliance.

Eminent practitioners from
healthcare and hospital systems
provide expert mentorship
throughout the program. Students
also undertake a compulsory
internship that bridges classroom
learning with real-world practice,
ensuring they gain practical exposure
and confidence to thrive in one of the
world’s most dynamic industries.
