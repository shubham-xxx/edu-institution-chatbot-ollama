MBA in Banking, Financial Services & Insurance (BFSI)

This program its the study of fundamentals as well as concepts in the Banking, Financial and
Insurance sector. The program details on the fundamental concepts of Management with
specialised training in subjects such as, Banking, Financial Services, Insurance, Corporate
aspects of Management, Investment Banking, Wealth Management and Core & Retail

Banking Operations.

The 2-Year MBA in Banking, Financial Services and
Insurance program has been designed, keeping in view
the industry changing dynamics of BFSI industry, and
has been divided into 3 basic modules:

Banking

This module covers key areas such as core banking,
retail, private, corporate, investment, and cards. While
retail and core banking are sometimes used
interchangeably, core banking primarily includes
transaction accounts, loans, mortgages, and payment
services.

Financial Services

This module focuses on services such as stock
brokering, mutual funds, and payment gateways.
Payment gateways allow retailers to process e-
commerce, in-app, and point-of-sale transactions
across multiple payment methods. Often, a single
payment gateway integrates several acquiring banks
and payment options into one seamless system.
Innovations in this area include real-time banking
payments and diverse digital payment solutions.

Insurance

This module covers both life and general insurance.
Representing over one-third of the financial sector,
insurance provides essential risk management tools
for individuals and businesses. You will explore the
critical role of insurance in the financial system and
the opportunities for innovation within this sector.

BFSI Industry in India

The BFSI sector in India comprises commercial banks,
insurance firms, non-banking financial institutions, co-
operatives, pension funds, mutual funds, and other
financial organisations. Over the past 15 years, the
industry has undergone significant reforms and
remains a key driver of India’s inclusive growth.
Regulatory initiatives have facilitated the entry of new
players, while government reforms continue to
liberalise and strengthen the sector.
