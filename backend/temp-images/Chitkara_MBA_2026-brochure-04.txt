CHITKARA UNIVERSITY
PUNJAB

Chitkara University is a UGC recognised University
with the right to confer degrees as per the Sections
2(f) and 22(1) of the UGC Act, 1956 and is established
by the Punjab State Legislature under,

“The Chitkara University Act”.
