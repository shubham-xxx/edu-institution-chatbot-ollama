» Development Centre I

Powered by Apple and Infosys

The iOS Student Development Centre at Chitkara University, powered by Apple and Infosys,
industry leaders shaping the world’s most advanced mobile ecosystems, offers every student
the chance to experience world-class innovation at their fingertips. Students gain access to
Apple’s products, tools and resources, strengthening their creativity and confidence to build
meaningful solutions. Through hands-on product development journeys, real-world projects,
expert mentorship, and global opportunities such as the WWDC Swift Student Challenge,
students develop solid technical skills and a thoughtful approach to problem-solving while
engaging with two industry giants defining the future of mobile technology.
