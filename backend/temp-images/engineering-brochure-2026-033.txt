Global Mobility of our

Engineering Graduates

Our Engineering graduates have the option to study the first 2 years of Engineering programs at Chitkara University
campus and then complete their Degree at a partner global University. Chitkara University offers study abroad

programs across the world.

Through the years annually more than 500+ Chitkara Engineering students experience global mobility across
75+ Universities on internships, summer school and semester exchange.
