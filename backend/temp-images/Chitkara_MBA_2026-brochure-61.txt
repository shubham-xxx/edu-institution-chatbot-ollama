Specialisation in Public Health Management

The Public Health Management specialisation prepares students to address large-scale health challenges at community
and population levels, great for meaningful careers improving wellness across India. Industry collaborations with
ACCESS Health International and PATH provide real-world exposure to policy and program implementation.

It focuses on improving healthcare access, designing effective health programs, and shaping policies for public health
well-being. The curriculum includes epidemiology, biostatistics, health policy and planning, health economics, and public
health systems. Students develop research, data analysis, program management, and strategic planning skills to tackle

real-world health issues, preparing them for roles in government, NGOs, and international organisations, with a strong
emphasis on leadership and innovation.
