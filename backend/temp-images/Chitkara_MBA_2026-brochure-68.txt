2-Year Full-Time Program
MBA in Retail Management

Chitkara College of Sales & Marketing offers an industry-focused MBA in Retail Management
that blends core Marketing and Retailing courses to prepare students for leadership roles in
retail management, buying, product development and visual merchandising.

The Indian retail industry is one of the world’s most
dynamic and fast-growing sectors, emerging as a top
global destination. With consumer spending driving
economic growth, retail has become a key indicator
of market trends. This vibrant industry demands
professionals who combine strong management skills
with marketing insight to thrive in a competitive,
evolving marketplace.

Success in retail depends on understanding the end
consumer and crafting strategies that resonate with
their needs. As India’s retail market expands rapidly,
it continues to absorb a significant number of
graduates each year. Managers who can anticipate
trends, design innovative solutions and execute
effective strategies are highly sought after across
diverse retail domains.

MBA in Retail Management at Chitkara University
offers a comprehensive curriculum covering Service
Operations, Consumer Behaviour, Sales & Marketing
Management, Visual Merchandising, Supply Chain,
Branding, Negotiation Skills and Financial
Management. This integrated approach ensures
students gain both theoretical grounding and practical
insights into the retail business landscape.

The program emphasises experiential learning
through workshops, consultancy projects, practical
assignments, dissertations and internships. Graduates
acquire applied skills and industry-ready confidence,
positioning them to excel across the retail sector. With
growing opportunities in India’s FMCG & retail sectors,
alumni step into rewarding roles in management,
strategy, sales and brand development.
