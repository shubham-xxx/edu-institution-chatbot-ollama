2-Year Full Time Program

MBA in Rural Management
with Minor in Artificial Intelligence for Business

Chitkara Business School’s MBA in Rural Management equips students with a comprehensive
understanding of rural markets, development and the intersection of business and rural enterprises.
The program prepares students for leadership roles in rural sectors, with a strong emphasis on
sustainable growth and development.

India’s expanding rural economy
continues to create fresh
opportunities for businesses and
professionals who possess
specialised skills and deep
contextual awareness. This program
directly responds to the increasing
significance of rural and semi-urban
markets, where development and
changing consumer preferences
have opened avenues for innovation,
leadership and rewarding career
advancement in numerous sectors.

The curriculum nurtures students

to design responsible, ethical
strategies, appreciate diverse
consumer mindsets and proactively
adapt to evolving rural market trends
and realities. Through focused study
in areas ranging from agri-business
to entrepreneurship, graduates learn
how to balance commercial

objectives with sustained social
impact within community-driven
projects and initiatives, making a
lasting difference.

Emerging as a unique domain of
expertise, rural management now
attracts leading companies operating
across FMCG, financial services,
retail and developmental
organisations. Graduates can explore
varied roles in corporates, NGOs,
microfinance institutions, or social
enterprises, contributing to rural
progress, livelihoods and broader
sustainable development efforts for
the country and beyond.

The minor in Artificial Intelligence
in Business further enhances each
student's career readiness and
versatility. Students build practical
skills in automation, data analytics
and emerging technologies,
equipping themselves to use digital
tools for market analysis, operational
efficiency and customer
insight—providing a distinct
advantage in an increasingly
technology-driven business
environment.
