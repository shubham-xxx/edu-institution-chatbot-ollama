Chitkara College of Sales & Marketing offers three specialised MBA programs — MBA in
Retail Management, MBA in Pharmaceutical Management, and MBA in Banking, Financial
Services & Insurance (BFSI). Designed to provide students with both vertical and horizontal
career growth, these programs come with 100% placement assistance through leading
partner organisations, ensuring a strong career start in high-demand industry domains.

practical knowledge alongside
strong theoretical foundations.
This structure maximises

them to adapt confidently to
diverse business challenges
across sectors and industries.
