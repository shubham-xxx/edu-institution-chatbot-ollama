MBA in Finance

Super Specialisations

After the completion of the 1st Semester of Finance, students will have the option to pursue
from following niche areas of core finance industry.

Investment Banking, Corporate Finance

& Wealth Management with CXO NextGen
India’s only MBA in Finance mentored by 3,000+ CXOs
from top firms, investment banks and corporates.
Designed through the CXO NextGen Centre of Excellence,
it focuses on investment banking, corporate finance

and wealth management—preparing future-ready
finance leaders with direct mentorship from senior
industry professionals.

Environment, Social and

Governance (ESG)

This specialisation builds expertise in integrating
environmental, social and governance factors into
investment, risk and corporate strategies. Students gain
skills to align businesses with sustainability, responsible
finance and ethical decision-making—an increasingly
critical advantage in today’s global financial landscape.

Global Financial Market Operations
with CISI

Gain expertise in foreign exchange, cross-border
investments and securities operations with global
certification from CISI. This specialisation equips
students for careers in international finance by
developing skills in portfolio management, risk
analysis and global market strategies, tailored for
the interconnected world economy.

Risk Management, Audit & Assurance

with Grant Thornton

Students master frameworks in risk identification,
internal audits and compliance. This specialisation
prepares them to design effective control systems,
safeguard organisations and excel as auditors,
compliance officers or risk managers, strengthening
financial resilience in any business environment.

AML, Mortgage and Commercial Lending
with WNS

This industry-driven specialisation builds capabilities
in anti-money laundering, mortgage underwriting
and business lending. Students learn to safeguard
institutions, assess property financing and structure
commercial loans. With WNS collaboration, it opens
global career opportunities in compliance, credit
analysis, underwriting and corporate lending.

Fintech with Paytm

At the intersection of finance and technology, this
specialisation covers blockchain, Al, digital payments
and emerging innovations. Students gain both technical
and financial acumen, preparing to transform banking
and financial services with cutting-edge, technology-
driven solutions.

Applied Finance with Deloitte

This super specialisation builds expertise in financial
management, quantitative finance, investments and
risk analysis. With Deloitte collaboration, students gain
hands-on exposure through case studies, simulations
and projects, using Al-driven analytics to enhance
decision-making. Graduates are prepared for roles in
consulting, portfolio analysis and corporate finance,
including financial analyst, portfolio manager or CFO.

Financial Crime Risk Management
(FCRM) with Genpact

Super-specialise in Financial Crime Risk Management
help you master cutting-edge risk mitigation, Al-powered
fraud detection, KYC/AML compliance and governance
frameworks. Become a sought-after expert who protects
institutions, clients and society from financial crime,
enabled by Genpact’s deep domain and technology
leadership. Learn directly from industry leaders shaping
the global risk landscape.

Financial Planning with Financial

Planning Standards Board (FPSB)

Aligned with the Chartered Financial Planner (CFP)
certification, this specialisation trains students to excel
as financial planners, wealth managers and advisors.
Combining theoretical learning with practical training,
case Studies and industry insights, the program prepares
graduates for roles in investment advisory, retirement
planning, wealth management and risk planning in
diverse financial settings.

Enterprise Risk Management with
Institute of Risk Management (IRM), UK

Chitkara Business School has signed an MoU with the
Institute of Risk Management (IRM), UK, the world’s
leading professional body for Enterprise Risk
Management. This specialisation equips MBA students
with globally recognised risk skills, combining theory,
practical learning and case studies.
