Specialisation in Digital Health Management

The Digital Health Management specialisation equips students to work at the intersection of healthcare, technology
and data, perfect for tech-savvy graduates entering India’s digital health revolution. Curriculum developed in close
collaboration with APCOGSYS for cutting-edge, industry-aligned training.

It focuses on how digital tools, health informatics, and emerging technologies like Al transform healthcare delivery,
improve patient outcomes, and enable smarter, informed decision-making. Students learn healthcare data analytics,
digital health systems, telemedicine, interoperability, and Al applications while building analytical, technical, and
strategic skills. Graduates are ready for high-demand roles in digital health companies, health IT firms, consulting
and innovation-driven organisations.
