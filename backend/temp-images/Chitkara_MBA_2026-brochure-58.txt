Program Structure

MBA in Healthcare Management at Chitkara Business School is designed to provide
students with advanced business management skills required to organise and manage

complex healthcare delivery systems.

As healthcare continues to evolve, leaders require the expertise to drive innovation and develop new business models.
Managers must navigate environments shaped by technology and regulation. The MBA in Healthcare Management at
Chitkara Business School equips graduates through interdisciplinary learning, strategic insight, innovation and real-world
projects, preparing them to manage health systems, lead organisations, or launch ventures with confidence.
