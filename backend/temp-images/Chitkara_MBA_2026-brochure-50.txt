This unique program merges the principles of

A Dual Focus on Business and Technology

Our curriculum builds a strong foundation in
business administration while offering in-depth
knowledge of data science and artificial
intelligence. You'll master traditional MBA
subjects such as leadership, finance, marketing
and operations alongside cutting-edge
technologies like machine learning, predictive
analytics and Al-driven decision-making.

Advanced Courses in Data Science and Al
Specialised courses include Machine Learning
and Deep Learning, Big Data Analytics, Natural
Language Processing (NLP}, Data Visualisation
and Insightful Storytelling and Al for Business
Strategy and Decision-Making, equipping you
to optimise operations, enhance customer
experiences and drive innovation.

Real-World Application and Capstone Projects
Hands-on learning ensures theory comes to life.
You'll work on real-world projects applying Al and
data science to solve complex business problems.
A capstone project with industry partners or
startup initiatives provides practical experience

in implementing Al-driven solutions.

Industry-Relevant Skill Development

The program also builds practical expertise in
high-demand areas such as Python, R, SQL,
Tableau and Power BI.
