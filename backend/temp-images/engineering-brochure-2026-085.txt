CAREER OPPORTUNITIES

Electrical Engineers are in high demand in India. In the recent years, from homes to companies, there is a necessity

of electricity to function, offering numerous opportunities to Electrical Engineers. These Engineers can work in Atomic
Power Plants, Hydel or Thermal Power Plants. Job opportunities are ample in both Private & Public sectors like
Railways, Civil Aviation, Electricity Board and Utility Companies, Electrical Design and Consultancy Firms and all types
of manufacturing industries.

Companies like ABB, Bajaj International Private Ltd, Crompton Greaves Limited, Siemens Ltd, Reliance Power Ltd,
Oil and Natural Gas Corporation (ONGC), Bharat Heavy Electricals Limited (BHEL), Steel Authority of India Limited
(SAIL), Coal India Limited (CIL), Power Grid Corporation of India Limited (PGCIL), Centre for Electronics Design &
Technology and Wipro Lighting are the biggest employers hiring Electrical Engineers.

Career opportunities abound across industries as Specialists, Technologists, Engineers or Managers in:
Factory Automation | Power Engineering | Energy Management | Facility Management

Operations Management | Sustainable Design & Solutions | Entrepreneurship
