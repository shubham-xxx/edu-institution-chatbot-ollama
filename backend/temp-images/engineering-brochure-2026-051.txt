AUSTRALIAN ENGINEERING
PATHWAY PROGRAMS

Chitkara University, in academic mentorship with Deakin University,
Australia, offers exclusive 2+2 pathway programs in Software
Engineering and Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (Al & ML).

Students can begin their journey in India by enrolling in either the
B.E. in Software Engineering or the B.E. in Artificial Intelligence &
Machine Learning (Al & ML) at Chitkara University. After completing
the first two years, they have the option to seamlessly transfer, with
100% credit recognition (subject to statutory requirements), into the
third year of Deakin University’s coveted 4-year Bachelor of Software
Engineering (Honours) or Bachelor of Artificial Intelligence (Honours)
programs in Australia.

Through this model, students gain access to Deakin’s advanced
applied engineering curriculum and mentorship from accomplished
international faculty while studying in India. The programs are
designed to prepare future-ready professionals skilled in building
disruptive technologies and developing innovative solutions that
address tomorrow’s challenges. Students can explore diverse

and emerging areas such as robotics, algorithms, programming,
Artificial Intelligence systems and software architecture, while
working with state-of-the-art facilities.
