CODE EXPERIENCE
CENTER ON CAMPUS

The CodeXperience Center (CEC) Lab at
Chitkara University, has been developed in
partnership with Capgemini, world’s leading
Al-powered technology and consulting
companies known for steering digital
transformation across global industries.

This center is designed to foster real-world
competencies in emerging technologies, enabling
students to gain future-ready skills through
immersive training programs, hands-on live
projects, and expert mentorship from industry
professionals. By bridging the gap between
academia and industry, the center empowers
learners to thrive in the dynamic digital
landscape and become catalysts for innovation.
