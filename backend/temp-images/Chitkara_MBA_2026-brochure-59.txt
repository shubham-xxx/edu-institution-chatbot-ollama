Specialisation in Hospital Management

The Hospital Management specialisation prepares students to efficiently manage hospitals and healthcare facilities. It
focuses on building strong capabilities in hospital operations, patient care systems, quality standards, and healthcare
administration, ideal for stable careers in India’s booming hospital networks and medical tourism sector.

Students gain practical knowledge of how hospitals function, from clinical services to support systems, ensuring smooth
delivery of care. The curriculum covers hospital operations, healthcare quality, patient experience, medical laws and
ethics, accreditation standards, and healthcare finance. With hands-on learning, case studies, and industry exposure,
graduates develop leadership, communication, and decision-making skills for managing teams in complex healthcare
environments.
